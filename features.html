OCTASCENE V1

One production system. Six connected stages.

V1 takes long-form documentary production from project setup and narration through planning, generation, validation, recovery, cost tracking and organized export.

01

Project & Script

Project manager, full-script input/import, persistent local production state.

02

Narration & Timing

ElevenLabs, local Chatterbox, and actual audio timing analysis.

03

AI Director & Storyboard

Gemini scene planning, shot logic and an editable storyboard before rendering.

04

Generate & Review

Image queues, browser review, 1–4 selections per shot and video queues.

05

Timeline & Validation

Lightweight narration timeline, linked assets, trims, coverage gaps and duration checks.

06

Reliability & Export

Retry/resume, persistent state, cost tracking and organized final assets.
