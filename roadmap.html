PRODUCT ROADMAP

From production workflow to an intelligent documentary directing system.

OctaScene evolves in three stages: first, a dependable long-form production pipeline; then a narrative-aware AI director; finally, a broader production system with advanced automation, reusable direction intelligence, and deeper asset workflows.

V1 · Production FoundationV2 · Intelligent DirectorV3 · Advanced Production System
V1
CORE PRODUCTION WORKFLOW

Build the complete long-form documentary pipeline.

V1 focuses on getting a creator from script to organized production assets with narration timing, AI shot planning, media generation, review, timeline coverage, cost visibility, and reliable resume/retry behavior.

Project & Script

  • Project manager and persistent local project state
  • Full-script input and TXT/JSON import
  • Structured chapters, sections, scenes, and shots
  • Editable shot settings and project-level configuration

Narration & Timing

  • ElevenLabs narration support
  • Local Chatterbox narration support
  • Actual generated-audio timing analysis
  • Scene and shot timing derived from narration

AI Planning & Storyboard

  • Gemini-based scene and shot planning
  • Narrative-aware shot breakdowns
  • Editable storyboard and shot metadata
  • Per-shot image and motion prompts

Image & Video Generation

  • Batch image generation queues
  • Browser review and selection of 1–4 images per shot
  • Batch image-to-video generation
  • Provider/model settings, quality, seed, concurrency, retries

Timeline & Validation

  • Lightweight editor-style timeline
  • Main narration track and linked scene/shot blocks
  • Trim, extend, reorder within production constraints
  • Coverage gaps, overlaps, and duration validation

Reliability, Cost & Export

  • Retry/resume with persistent state
  • Budget and generation cost tracking
  • Budget-aware use of cheaper vs premium routes
  • Organized export of selected final assets
V1 goal: make long-form AI production manageable, inspectable, and repeatable instead of a collection of disconnected prompt tools.
V2
NARRATIVE-AWARE AI DIRECTOR

Turn OctaScene from a workflow tool into a directing system.

V2 adds persistent creative direction, deeper narrative understanding, reusable directing intelligence, AI assistance throughout the app, and richer documentary-specific shot systems.

Project Direction Bible

  • First-class editable creative-direction object
  • Genre, emotional arc, shot grammar, average shot duration
  • Camera, graphic, map, archive, sound, transition rules
  • Pattern-interrupt strategy and chapter-specific direction

Narrative-Aware Directing

  • Understand setup, reveal, context, process, consequence, transition, and other narrative functions
  • Select shot grammar based on meaning, not only prompt text
  • Adjust pacing, camera behavior, asset type, sound and graphics by narrative role
  • Long-form continuity across chapters and scenes

Custom Direction Profiles

  • Analyze reference channels/videos for production grammar
  • Learn average cut duration and shot-type distribution
  • Analyze maps, archives, documents, graphics, B-roll, transitions, music intensity
  • Capture hook structure and chapter structure without copying visuals

Documentary Shot Systems

  • Map and GeoLayers-style shot workflows
  • Archive/document/public-domain asset shot types
  • Claims, dates, geography, evidence and source-aware visual planning
  • Reusable graphics and explanatory visual systems

AI Assistant, Chat & Agent

  • In-app AI assistant that understands the project and product
  • Context-aware help, troubleshooting, and workflow guidance
  • Agentic actions across project setup, generation queues, review, and fixes
  • Suggestions based on project state, costs, coverage, and narrative intent

Provider & Budget Intelligence

  • Bring your own provider, model, and API keys
  • Route shots by price, speed, style, quality, and availability
  • Reserve premium models for hero shots
  • Use lower-cost routes for drafts, B-roll, and iterations
V2 goal: differentiate OctaScene as a documentary director that understands what each part of a story is doing and keeps creative direction consistent across the whole film.
V3
ADVANCED PRODUCTION SYSTEM

Expand from directing individual projects into a reusable production intelligence layer.

V3 builds on the V2 director with more automation, scalable production workflows, stronger reuse across projects, and deeper finishing, collaboration, and orchestration capabilities.

Reusable Production Intelligence

  • Reusable direction packages across projects
  • Project templates and channel-level production systems
  • Learned preferences for pacing, shot grammar, graphics and asset mixes
  • Versioned production rules and style evolution

Advanced Agentic Production

  • Multi-step production agents for planning, generating, reviewing, and repairing coverage
  • Autonomous retry and alternate-route selection within user limits
  • Quality, budget, and deadline-aware generation decisions
  • Human approval gates for important creative decisions

Richer Asset & Research Workflows

  • Broader archival, map, document, public-domain and research asset integration
  • Source-aware evidence tracking for documentary visuals
  • Automatic asset categorization, provenance, and reuse
  • Better matching of claims to supporting visuals

Finishing & Editorial Depth

  • More advanced timeline operations and sequence assembly
  • Expanded transitions, sound, graphics, maps, and motion systems
  • Stronger export to downstream editors and finishing tools
  • Automated continuity, pacing, and quality checks

Collaboration & Team Workflows

  • Shared project review and approvals
  • Role-based creative review
  • Comments, shot status, handoff and revision tracking
  • Production visibility across larger projects or teams

Commercial Platform Layer

  • Usage and Intelligence/Intel accounting if credits are introduced
  • Team/project budgets and cost controls
  • Provider marketplaces or preset route packs
  • Scalable hosted and local/hybrid deployment options
V3 goal: make OctaScene a broader operating system for long-form AI documentary production, where creative direction, generation, research, review, cost, and finishing work together.
