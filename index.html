WHY OCTASCENE

AI generators make clips.
OctaScene directs a film.

Most AI video tools start with a prompt and end with an isolated asset. OctaScene starts with the story. It keeps narrative intent, narration timing, visual direction, scene coverage, and human review connected across an entire long-form production.

Script→Narrative intent→Shot plan→Generate→Review→Timing + coverage→Finished assets
01 / STORY-AWARE

Direct from narrative meaning

Scenes are treated as setup, transition, reveal, consequence, context and more—not just text prompts. Shot choices, pacing, and visual treatment can follow what each moment is doing in the story.

02 / CONSISTENT

One Direction Bible for the whole film

Keep visual style, camera language, narrative voice, palette, pacing rules, and do/don't guidance in a shared production direction instead of reinventing the look for every generation.

03 / LONG-FORM

Narration is the timeline, not an afterthought

OctaScene works from actual narration timing, maps scenes to time, exposes gaps and overlaps, and validates whether generated media really covers the documentary from beginning to end.

04 / HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP

Generate options. You keep creative control.

Batch-generate, compare, select the best assets for each shot, retry where needed, and resume later. The system handles production scale without turning the documentary into a one-click black box.

Built for long-form documentary production — where story continuity matters more than generating a single impressive clip.See the complete workflow →
CONTROL THE COST, NOT JUST THE OUTPUT

Use the best model where it matters. Use the cheaper route where it doesn't.

Long-form production can require hundreds of generations. OctaScene is designed around budget-aware production: track spend, choose providers and models by shot, reserve premium generation for important moments, and use lower-cost routes for drafts, iterations, and supporting coverage.

PROJECT BUDGET$47 / $100
Drafts → economical modelsHero shots → premium models
SMARTER ROUTING

Match cost to the shot

Not every transition needs the most expensive model. Route exploratory generations, B-roll, and iterations to lower-cost options, then spend more on shots where quality has the biggest impact.

BRING YOUR OWN PROVIDER

Your accounts. Your API keys.

Connect supported providers with your own credentials instead of being locked into a single bundled generation service. Keep control over provider pricing, quotas, and account usage.

MODEL CHOICE

Choose the model that fits the job

Switch between available image, video, voice, and planning models based on quality, speed, style, or price. OctaScene coordinates the production while you choose the engines.

VISIBLE SPEND

Know what production is costing

Track generation costs as the project grows, compare routes before repeating expensive jobs, and make informed decisions instead of discovering the bill after the documentary is finished.
